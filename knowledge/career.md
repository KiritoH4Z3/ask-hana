# Career timeline

## Studying
I did a bachelor's degree in geomatics engineering at a university in Sharjah, graduating in 2011. Geomatics is surveying, mapping and positioning, and I chose it because I liked the idea of a job where the answer is a number that someone can check. In 2013 I moved to Plymouth in the UK for a master's in hydrography, which I finished in 2014. It rained in 11 of my first 14 days there, and I counted.

## First years at sea
After the master's I joined Gulfline Marine Survey, a mid-sized survey contractor, and stayed for seven years. My first vessel was the MV Khor Falcon, working in the Gulf of Oman. I was the junior surveyor, which meant I carried the heavy cables and learned everyone's tea preferences. That is where I met the crewmate who taught me Malayalam.

## Arabian Gulf
In 2017 I moved to the RV Al Barid, a larger survey ship working in the Arabian Gulf. I became the party chief in 2018, the person responsible for the whole survey crew of nine. The longest trip I did was 38 days at sea without seeing land that was not a platform. I got very good at playing cards and not very good at sleeping.

## Leaving to start something
By 2021 I had spent seven years at sea and wanted to choose my own jobs and sleep in my own bed. I left Gulfline Marine Survey on good terms in March 2021 and founded Datum Line Surveys that autumn. The first year was the hardest: three clients, one borrowed boat, and a lot of spreadsheets.

## Things I am glad I did
Learning to process data myself, not just collect it. Taking my free diving course in 2017, between ships. And saying yes to a volunteer reef survey in 2022, which turned into the thing I lead every spring.

## Things I would do differently
I would have learned to code earlier. I wrote my first script in 2019 to clean survey noise automatically, and it saved me about six hours a week. I would also have bought a better pair of sea boots in my first year.

## Certifications
I hold a hydrographic surveyor certificate at Category A, which is the full professional level, plus a small-vessel skipper licence for our own launch.
