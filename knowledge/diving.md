# Diving

## How I started
I started scuba diving in 2016 on a work posting, mostly because the crew did it on days off and I was tired of watching from the deck. I took my free diving course in 2017 in Dibba, on the east coast, and I have been slowly getting deeper ever since. Free diving is breath-hold diving with no tank. It is quiet in a way that scuba never is.

## Personal bests
My personal best in constant weight is 32 m. Constant weight means you swim down and up under your own power, without pulling on the rope and without changing the weight you carry. I reached 32 m in October 2024 off Dibba, after being stuck at 30 m for most of a year. My free immersion best is 35 m, where you are allowed to pull yourself along the rope, and my static apnea best is 4 minutes 10 seconds, which I did lying face down in a pool. Do not confuse the numbers: the 32 m one is the swim, the 35 m one uses the rope.

## The logbook
I log every dive by hand. My logbook has 412 dives in it, across four notebooks, starting with a very shaky entry from 2016. Of those, about 290 are scuba and the rest are free dives. I write the date, site, depth, time, water temperature, and one line. My favourite line reads: "Turtle looked at me like I owed it money."

## Where I dive
Most of my diving is on the east coast: Dibba Rock, the reefs off Khor Fakkan, and the wreck sites near Fujairah. Water temperature drops to 23 degrees in February and climbs to 33 in August. Visibility is usually between 8 and 20 metres, and after a storm it can be zero for days.

## Safety rules I follow
- I never free dive alone. I always dive with a buddy who watches me at the surface.
- I do not hyperventilate. I breathe slowly and calmly before a dive.
- I stop for the day if I feel anything unusual, even if the water is perfect.

## Why 35 m is not my headline number
People always ask why I lead with 32 m and not 35 m. It is because constant weight is the standard people compare, and because the rope makes the other one slightly easier.

## What is next
I am training to reach 35 m in constant weight by March 2027. My coach says it is reasonable. My lungs say they will think about it.
