# Reading and music

## What I read
I read about 30 books a year, mostly on ships and planes. I keep a list, because I forget what I thought of a book within a month. I like books where a person is obsessed with something very specific and the author lets them explain it.

## Favourite books
"The Sea Around Us" by Rachel Carson is the book that made me love the ocean as a subject, and I first read it on the MV Khor Falcon in 2014. "Longitude" by Dava Sobel is a short, funny account of the clockmaker who solved the problem of knowing where you are at sea. For fiction I keep going back to "Season of Migration to the North" by Tayeb Salih. I read it first in Arabic, and I reread it in 2023.

## What I am reading now
In November 2026 I am halfway through "The Hidden Life of Trees" by Peter Wohlleben, which a friend lent me, and I have not given it back yet. I know that makes me a bad friend.

## What I listen to
While working I listen to Fairuz, mostly her older records, and I play "Kifak Inta" more often than I should admit. On long boat transits I listen to Umm Kulthum, because a single song can last as long as one survey line. When I process data I put on Nils Frahm and the piano helps me stay patient.

## The oud connection
I started the oud because of Umm Kulthum, so every wrong note I play is, in a way, her fault. My teacher is a patient online instructor, and we meet over video once a week for 45 minutes on Thursday evenings. I wrote more about the oud on the hobbies page.

## Podcasts
Two podcasts: one about ship wrecks, and one about maths that I understand about 60 percent of. I listen at 1.25 speed. I do not recommend it for the maths one.

## A small rule
I do not finish books that bore me. Life is short and the seabed is big.
