# Travel

## Home base
I travel for work more than for pleasure, and what I most like about travel is coming back to Fujairah. When I am not on a boat, I like the road from Fujairah to Dibba, which takes about 55 minutes and passes more mountain than any road should be allowed to.

## Two mountains
People mix these up, so here is the short version. Jebel Jais, in Ras Al Khaimah, is 1,934 metres high and is the highest peak in the UAE. I go there for the night sky. Jebel Shams, in Oman, is 3,009 metres high and I went there in January 2024 for a two-day trek along the rim of the canyon. I do astrophotography at Jebel Jais, not Jebel Shams.

## Kerala
In 2018 I visited Kerala, in India, for two weeks, to use my Malayalam with the family of my crewmate. They were very patient with me. I ate a banana-leaf lunch, got lost twice in Kochi, and learned that the word for "I do not understand" is the one I use most.

## The UK
I lived in Plymouth from 2013 to 2014 for my master's, and went back for a week in 2022 to see the harbour in sunshine, which I had never done. It was raining. A local told me that was sunshine.

## Lofoten, Norway
In March 2023 I travelled to the Lofoten islands in Norway to see the northern lights. We saw them on the third of five nights, and I took 212 photos, of which 9 were good. It was minus 6 degrees, and my camera battery lasted 40 minutes.

## Socotra
In 2019, on a work trip, I spent 10 days off Socotra in Yemen, on a research ship. I saw the dragon blood trees from a distance and the seabed from much closer. It remains my favourite place that I have only seen from a boat.

## Travel rules
Carry one film camera. Eat where the drivers eat. Never check a bag that has a sensor in it.
