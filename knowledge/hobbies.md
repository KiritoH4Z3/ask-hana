# Hobbies

## The short list
Outside work I free dive, photograph the night sky, restore old film cameras, bake sourdough, and learn the oud. I keep a handwritten dive logbook. People think this is a lot of hobbies. I think it is what happens when you work on a boat for years and have nothing to do on the long transits except plan your free time.

## Astrophotography in the Hajar Mountains
The Hajar Mountains behind Fujairah have some of the darkest skies in the country that are still reachable by car. My favourite spot is a gravel pull-off near Jebel Jais, at 1,934 metres, where on a clear new-moon night the Milky Way is bright enough to cast a faint shadow. I shoot with a Nikon Z6 and a star tracker, and a typical image is 90 two-minute frames stacked together. My best image so far is the Andromeda galaxy, from November 2025, built from 4 hours of exposure.

## Restoring film cameras
I restore 1970s film cameras. I have restored 9 so far. My favourites are an Olympus OM-1, made in 1974, and a Canon AE-1 from 1977, which was the first camera I rebuilt. The OM-1 needed new light seals and a cleaned shutter. The AE-1 needed a new capacitor and a lot of patience, and I broke one tiny screw doing it. I load a roll into one of them on every trip.

## Sourdough
My date-and-cardamom sourdough has taken me about three years to get right. The details are on the food page.

## The oud
I started learning the oud in November 2024, so I am two years in. I am still bad at it, and I would like that on the record. I can play one tune, slowly, with only two wrong notes, and my cat leaves the room when I practise. I practise 20 minutes a day, most days.

## The dive logbook
I keep a handwritten dive logbook. As of November 2026 it has 412 dives logged, in four notebooks. I write depth, time, water temperature, and one line about what I saw. More on the diving page.

## Which hobby is the main one?
If I could keep only one it would be free diving. If I had to save only one object from a fire it would be the dive logbook.
