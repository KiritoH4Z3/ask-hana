# Side projects

## Tidebook
Tidebook is an open-source tide-table app that I maintain. It started in 2023 when I got tired of tide tables that were wrong by twenty minutes on the east coast. Tidebook takes harmonic constants for a station, predicts the tide for any date, and prints a pocket-sized table you can fold into a logbook. It is written in Python with a small web front end, and it has about 640 stars on GitHub, which is both more than I expected and fewer than I secretly hoped.

## How Tidebook works
Tides are the sum of a few dozen sine waves, each with its own period, driven by the moon and sun. Tidebook stores 37 harmonic constituents per station and adds them up. For Fujairah the largest is the principal lunar semidiurnal, which has a period of about 12.42 hours. Predictions for the next year take under a second to compute.

## Who uses it
Divers, fishers, a couple of sailing schools, and one person in Norway who writes to me every few months about a station in Tromso. Version 3.1 came out in June 2026. Version 3.2 is planned for December 2026 and will add a printable week view.

## The reef photo survey
Every spring I lead a volunteer reef photo survey. It began in 2022 with 6 volunteers. In April 2026 we had 14 volunteers, laid 38 transects along the reef off Khor Fakkan, and took 2,210 photos. Each volunteer swims a 25 metre tape with a camera held at a fixed height and takes overlapping photos along it, about 58 per tape. Back on land we score the photos for live coral cover.

## What we found
Average live coral cover on our transects was 31 percent in 2022 and 27 percent in 2026. That is a small drop over four years, within the range I would call noise, and I say so every time someone asks me for a dramatic number. The photos are shared with a university marine group, and the names of volunteers are only shared with their permission.

## Why I do them
The studio pays the bills, but these two projects are where I learn the most. Tidebook keeps my code honest, and the reef survey is the one week of the year when I am in the water with people who are all, for once, worse at it than I am.
